📡What is UUFNN Media Hub?▶
Write one announcement — get tailored versions for every channel, social and traditional — then email each version to the right volunteer or contact.

Channels

Social: Facebook, Instagram, X, Bluesky, Email newsletter, Bulletin
Traditional: Newspaper (AP release), Radio (60-sec advisory), TV advisory, Formal press release

Contacts

Social volunteers — congregation members who manage platforms
Media contacts — journalists and stations (require org name)
✏️Compose — writing, generating & the library▶

New announcement

Tap 📄 New to clear the form and start fresh. If you have unsaved work you'll be asked to confirm.

Save to library

Tap 💾 Save to library to store the current announcement (text + all generated versions) in the library. The library holds up to the number set in Settings (default 20). When full, the oldest entry is dropped after confirmation.

Library

Tap 📂 Library to browse saved announcements. Load any entry back into Compose with one tap, or delete entries you no longer need.

Generating

Use the channel filter to choose which channels to generate — or leave all on for all 10. Click Generate. Takes 10–20 seconds.

Editing

Tap ✏️ Edit on any generated block to edit it in place. Tap ✅ Done to save. Edited versions are what gets sent.
👥Contacts — managing your roster▶

Adding a contact

Tap + Add contact. Choose Social volunteer or Media outlet, fill in the details, and assign channels.

Demo contacts

Demo contacts (blue "demo" badge) are pre-loaded. Use Reload demo contacts to restore them.

Deleting

Tap 🗑 and confirm. Cannot be undone.

Editing

Delete and re-add to update a contact's information.
📤Distribute — selecting & sending▶

Filter & select

Use All / Social / Media filter chips to narrow the list, then check individual contacts. Select all / Select none work within the current filter.

Sending

Tap Email selected contacts. Your email client opens a pre-addressed draft for each selected contact — staggered 400ms apart. Nothing sends automatically.

What's in the email

Each contact receives only their assigned channel versions. Edits made in Compose are included.
📋Sent Log▶
Each time you distribute an announcement, a record is saved here — showing the headline, date, recipients, channels, and the full text sent to each person.

Expanding an entry

Tap Show content to see the full text sent to that contact.

Note on delivery

The log records that an email draft was opened in your email client, not that it was sent or delivered. Confirm in your email client's Sent folder.
📝What each channel version looks like▶

Facebook

2–3 warm paragraphs, full details, call to action. ~200–280 words.

Instagram

3–4 punchy sentences + 8–10 hashtags.

X / Twitter

Hard limit 270 characters including hashtags.

Bluesky

Hard limit 290 characters, warm tone.

Email newsletter

Subject line, greeting, 3–4 paragraphs, contact info, sign-off.

Bulletin insert

4 lines max, print-friendly, no URLs.

Newspaper

AP-style release, headline in caps, dateline, "###", media contact block.

Radio

~150-word 60-second read-aloud, written for the ear.

TV

WHAT/WHEN/WHERE/WHO/VISUAL OPPORTUNITIES, story angle, contact block.

Press release

Formal release with quote, boilerplate, full contact block.
⚙️Settings▶

Backup & Restore

Export backup downloads a JSON file with all your contacts, library entries, sent log, and settings. Save it to your files app or email it to yourself as a safe copy.

Restore from backup reads a previously exported JSON file and replaces all current data. A summary shows what will be loaded before you confirm.

Library size limit

Default is 20. You can set any number from 5 to 100 in the Settings tab. When you save a new announcement and the library is full, the oldest entry is dropped (after confirmation).

Clear library

Removes all saved announcements. Your current draft and contacts are not affected.

Reset all data

Clears everything — contacts, library, sent log, and draft. Use with caution.
💡Tips & best practices▶
• Give the AI everything — dates, times, location, what to bring, rain plan. More detail = better versions.

• Edit before you send — always review X/Bluesky character counts and double-check dates and names.

• Use channel filters — regenerate just social channels without losing your traditional media versions.

• Save recurring events to the library — load last year's picnic announcement, update the date, and regenerate.

• Stagger large sends — some mobile browsers block multiple tabs at once. If drafts don't all open, tap Send again for missed ones.
🔒Privacy & data▶
• All data (contacts, library, sent log, draft) is stored only on this device via IndexedDB.

• When you Generate, your announcement text is sent to an AI service via a Cloudflare Worker proxy. The API key is held by the Worker — never exposed in the app.

• Emails are opened in your default email client. The app does not access or send email on your behalf.
🐛Troubleshooting▶

Generation fails or times out

• Check your internet connection.
• Confirm the Cloudflare Worker is deployed and WORKER_URL matches.
• Verify ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is set in the Worker's environment.

Debug log

Tap UUFNN Media Hub in the header five times to open the debug log.

Emails don't open

Ensure a default email app is configured on your device.
